1 MARKUP — THE PRICING FOUNDATION
Retailer: Cost = $420, Markup = 35%
SP = Cost × (1 + r)  |  r = (SP−C)÷C
Markup rate = $147 ÷ $420 = 35%
⚠ Markup base = Cost. Gross margin base = SP. Same profit ≠ same %.
2 GROSS MARGIN VS. MARKUP
Same product: SP = $567, Cost = $420, Profit = $147
GM = (SP−C)÷SP  |  SP = Cost÷(1−GM)
GM from markup: GM = r ÷ (1+r) = 0.35÷1.35 = 25.9%
3 DISCOUNT CHAIN & IVA
List: $567, d₁ = 15%, d₂ = 5%, IVA 16%
Full chain: List × (1−d₁) × (1−d₂) × 1.16
