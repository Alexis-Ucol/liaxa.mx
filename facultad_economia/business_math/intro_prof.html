1 ALGEBRA = A BUSINESS LANGUAGE
= writing relationships between business quantities
Variable → unknown or changing qty  q, p, n
Constant → fixed number in context  $500, 0.16
Expression → no equals sign  3x + 500
Equation → two sides balanced  3x + 500 = 1700
"How many items at $35 + $20 delivery = $160 paid?"

3 THE PROFIT EQUATION
P = R − C
Revenue − Total Cost
R = p · q  price × units
C = F + v·q  fixed + variable
→ P = (p−v)·q − F
(p−v) = margin per unit
"Digital firm: p=$120  v=$30  F=$4,500"
P = 90q − 4500  break-even at q=50
4 BREAK-EVEN & KEY IDEAS
Set P = 0, solve for q*:
0 = (p−v)·q − F
q* = F ÷ (p−v)
q < q* → loss   q > q* → profit
Bigger margin (p−v) → lower break-even → faster profits
